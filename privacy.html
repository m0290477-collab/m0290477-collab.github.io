Super Snake – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Super Snake ("the game", "we") collects when you play at billke.com or in the app, and how it is used. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your username, email address, and password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider), your player ID, and when you joined.
  • Profile: the avatar, bio, country, medals, skins, and privacy choices you set, and a profile photo if you upload one (other players can see it).
  • Gameplay: match results, scores, levels, coins, gems, items, league points, and similar game records, so your progress is saved and leaderboards work.
  • Social: friends, blocks, chat messages with friends, reports you send, and support messages you write to us.
  • Purchases: what you bought, the amount, and the payment status. Payments are handled by Stripe (or the app store); we never receive your full card number.
  • Technical: basic data needed to run and secure the service, such as sign-in timestamps, abuse-prevention rate limits, and error reports from the game (linked to your account) so we can fix crashes.
  • Approximate location (optional): only if you turn on Real weather & time, your device sends a rough location (about 10 km) directly to Open-Meteo to look up the local weather. We do not store it.

2. What stays on your device

Settings like language, sound, and controls, and a photo you choose for the "photo skin", are kept only on your device and are not uploaded.

3. How we use it

  • To run the game, save your progress, show leaderboards and player cards, and deliver purchases.
  • To keep the game fair and safe (anti-cheat, moderation of reports).
  • To answer your support messages.

4. What other players see

Your username, player ID, avatar, level, league tier, medals, and, if you allow it, your country, online status, and stats. You can change these in Settings › Privacy.

5. Service providers

Supabase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare (online match servers, hosting and delivery), GitHub Pages (hosting), Apple (purchases in the iOS app), Stripe (purchases on the website), and Open-Meteo (weather, only if you turn it on). They process data only to provide their service to us. We do not use advertising or tracking, and we do not share your data with data brokers.

6. Children

Online accounts are for players aged 13 and over, because the game has chat with friends. The game has no ads. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account in Settings › Account › Delete account, or ask us through Help & report a problem. The account is closed at once and, after a 7-day grace period (sign in to cancel), permanently erased with your profile, photo, progress, items, friends, chat, and error reports. Records we must keep by law (for example payment records) are kept without your account details.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change. Questions? Use Help & report a problem in the game or email m0290477@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Service.

  • अनुमानित लोकेशन (वैकल्पिक): सिर्फ़ तब, जब आप असली मौसम और समय चालू करें — आपका डिवाइस स्थानीय मौसम जानने के लिए अनुमानित लोकेशन (लगभग 10 किमी) सीधे Open-Meteo को भेजता है। हम इसे सेव नहीं करते।

2. आपके डिवाइस पर क्या रहता है

भाषा, आवाज़ और कंट्रोल जैसी सेटिंग्स, और "फ़ोटो वाली स्किन" के लिए चुनी गई फ़ोटो सिर्फ़ आपके डिवाइस पर रहती हैं और अपलोड नहीं होतीं।

3. हम इसका इस्तेमाल कैसे करते हैं

  • गेम चलाने, आपकी प्रगति सेव करने, लीडरबोर्ड और खिलाड़ी कार्ड दिखाने और ख़रीदारी देने के लिए।
  • गेम को निष्पक्ष और सुरक्षित रखने के लिए (एंटी-चीट, रिपोर्ट की समीक्षा)।
  • आपके सपोर्ट संदेशों का जवाब देने के लिए।

5. सेवा प्रदाता

Supabase (डेटाबेस और साइन-इन), Cloudflare (ऑनलाइन मैच सर्वर, होस्टिंग और डिलीवरी), GitHub Pages (होस्टिंग), Apple (iOS ऐप में ख़रीदारी), Stripe (वेबसाइट पर ख़रीदारी) और Open-Meteo (मौसम, सिर्फ़ चालू करने पर)। ये डेटा को सिर्फ़ हमें अपनी सेवा देने के लिए प्रोसेस करते हैं। हम विज्ञापन या ट्रैकिंग का इस्तेमाल नहीं करते, और आपका डेटा डेटा ब्रोकरों के साथ साझा नहीं करते।
