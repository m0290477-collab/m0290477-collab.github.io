Super Snake – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Super Snake runs entirely on your device. It has no servers, no online accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings and progress (such as language, player name, coins and skins) in your browser's or device's local storage so it is there next time you play. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser or deleting the app.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it is safe for players of all ages.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data, we will update this page before that happens.
