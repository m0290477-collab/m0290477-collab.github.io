Super Snake – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the Super Snake game on the web and on mobile devices (the "Game") collects, how it is used, where it is stored, how long it is kept and how you can delete it. The Game is run by its developer, Molier Dawood ("we", "us"). Please also read our Terms of Service.

1. Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. Your settings and progress (such as language, player name, coins and skins) and a random device key are saved in your browser's or device's local storage. If you play online as a guest, the game server also keeps the online play data described in section 4, linked to an ID derived from that device key. You can erase the data on your device at any time by clearing the site data in your browser or deleting the app.

2. Accounts and sign-in

Signing in is optional. It lets you keep your progress and recover it on another device.

  • Sign in with Google: Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and a unique Google account identifier with us. We use them to create and recognise your account, to show your name and picture in your profile if you choose, and to contact you about your account. We do not receive your Google password. Google's sign-in is also covered by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Email sign-in: we collect your email address and send you a one-time sign-in code or link. The code is stored only in hashed form and expires after 10 minutes.

3. Emails we send

We send only transactional emails, such as sign-in codes or links and messages about your account or a request you made. They are delivered through Resend, an email delivery service, which receives your email address and the content of the message in order to deliver it. We do not send marketing emails.

4. Online play data

When you play online, the game server stores what the Game needs to work:

  • your player name, player ID, profile (avatar, frame, level) and when you were last online;
  • game sessions and matches: when you join and leave rooms and the results of your matches;
  • scores, trophies, statistics and leaderboard entries (your name, ID and score are visible to other players on leaderboards);
  • coins, items you own and rewards you have claimed;
  • where the Game offers them: friends, friend requests, blocked players, game invites and private chat messages with friends (only the last 100 messages of each conversation are kept);
  • reports and feedback you send, and moderation records such as warnings, mutes and bans, to keep the Game fair and safe.

5. Technical data and server logs

Like most online services, our servers automatically record technical information when the Game connects to them: your IP address, device and browser type, the date and time of requests, and error reports (for example, when something in the Game crashes). We use this only to run the Game, keep it secure, prevent cheating and abuse, and fix problems.

6. Where your data is stored

  • Game server: a virtual private server (VPS) located in Oregon, USA, at srv.irqus.com.
  • Supabase: a managed database service we use to store account and game data.
  • Resend: delivers our transactional emails (see section 3).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website itself (billke.com).

These providers process data on our behalf to run the Game. If you play from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • Chat messages: only the last 100 messages of each conversation; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Sign-in codes: 10 minutes. Game invites: a few minutes.
  • Server logs: about 30 days, then deleted.
  • Backups: the game server is backed up once a day and each backup is kept for 7 days. After you delete your data, copies in backups are removed within 7 days as old backups are deleted.
  • Ban records: a record that holds only a player ID and the reason may be kept after deletion to prevent abuse.

8. Deleting your account and data

To delete your account and all data linked to it, email m0290477@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or include your player ID. We will delete your account, profile, progress, friends, chats, coins, items, sign-in links and leaderboard entries within 30 days and confirm by email. Where the Game offers it, you can also delete your account or your online data yourself in Settings. You can also remove Super Snake's access from your Google account settings at any time.

9. Purchases

The Game does not currently sell anything for real money. If the Game ever offers purchases, payments will be processed by the app store or payment provider, we will not receive your full card details, and we will keep only a record of the purchase (item, amount, date and transaction ID) to deliver it and handle refunds. This policy will be updated before purchases are offered.

10. What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies, analytics services or cross-site tracking. We share data only with the service providers listed above to run the Game, or when required by law.

11. Children

Super Snake is a general-audience game. Children under 13 (or the minimum age in their country) should use sign-in and online features only with a parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without that permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Connections to the Game use encryption (HTTPS/TLS), sign-in codes are stored hashed, and access to the servers is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data.

13. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or ask questions about how it is used, by contacting us. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have additional rights under local law. We will not treat you differently for using them.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new date, and for significant changes we will give notice in the Game before they take effect.
